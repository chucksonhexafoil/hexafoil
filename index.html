Before You Buy

WHY USED OEM

Because “new” doesn’t always mean “better fit.”

OEM parts were designed, tested, and manufactured for the vehicle they came from. For many repairs—especially trim, electronics, lighting, brackets, and interior pieces—a good used factory part can be the most direct replacement.

FAQ

Used-part basics.

What does OEM mean?

OEM means original equipment manufacturer. Here, it refers to factory-spec parts removed from vehicles rather than generic replacement parts made for the aftermarket.

How do I know a part will fit?

The safest starting point is an exact OEM part-number match, followed by a visual comparison of connectors, mounting points, and configuration. Vehicle year, make, and model alone may not be enough.

Are used parts perfect?

No. Used parts can have cosmetic wear, dirt, fading, scratches, corrosion, or other signs of service. The goal is to describe and photograph relevant condition clearly so you can make an informed purchase.

Do you sell complete engines or transmissions?

Inventory depends on the donor vehicle and what can be handled economically. The initial focus is on valuable shippable OEM components rather than promising every category.

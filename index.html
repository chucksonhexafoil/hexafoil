THE HEXAFOIL PROCESS

We do the work before you buy.

1

Document the donor

We start with the vehicle, not the loose part. Relevant donor details, configuration, mileage when available, and diagnostic or operational information create context for what comes off the car.

2

Test before removal

When a component can reasonably be checked in the vehicle, that is the best time to do it. We use operational checks, diagnostic information, or other practical evidence when available.

3

Curate what is worth saving

We do not need to list every piece. Components with questionable condition, excessive wear, damage, or poor reuse value can be passed over instead of pushed into inventory.

4

Document the exact part

OEM numbers, labels, connectors, mounting points, cosmetic flaws, and relevant condition details are photographed and described so the listing represents the actual item.

FAQ

What “tested” means here.

Does every part get tested?

No. Different components allow different levels of verification. When practical, we test or observe a part operating before removal. Some items can only be visually inspected or identified. The listing should state the level of testing or observation actually performed.

Why test parts before removal?

Many electrical and mechanical components are easiest to evaluate while they are still installed, powered, connected, and able to operate in the vehicle. Once removed, reproducing those conditions can be much harder.

What does curated inventory mean?

It means we do not treat every removable component as inventory. We focus on parts that appear worth reusing and where the time spent identifying, checking, documenting, and shipping them creates value for a buyer.

How do I know a part will fit?

The safest starting point is an exact OEM part-number match, followed by a visual comparison of connectors, mounting points, and configuration. Vehicle year, make, and model alone may not be enough.

Are used parts perfect?

No. Used parts can have cosmetic wear, dirt, fading, scratches, corrosion, or other signs of service. The goal is not to make used parts look new; it is to show and describe their relevant condition clearly.

Why buy through eBay?

eBay can handle the live listing, checkout, payment, shipping workflow, and marketplace buyer protections. Hexafoil is where we explain the sourcing, curation, testing, and documentation process behind those listings.
